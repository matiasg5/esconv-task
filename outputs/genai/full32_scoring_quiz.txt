=================================================================
For each item, read the conversation + target strategy, then score EACH
of the 4 responses (A/B/C/D) on two dimensions, 1-5 each (5=best):
  quality: overall naturalness, appropriateness, helpfulness as a
    supportive chat reply -- including tone (does it read like one
    person messaging another, not a preference for length or for/against
    an acknowledgment-style opener).
  strategy_adherence: how well the response executes the target
    strategy (not just whether it's on-topic).
Options are shuffled per item -- don't assume a fixed order. Score all 4,
independently -- this is NOT a ranking, each response gets its own numbers.
